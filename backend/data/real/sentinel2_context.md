# Sentinel-2 contextual layer

Source: ESA Copernicus Sentinel-2 water-body monitoring documentation.

Role in AquaPulse: spatial/contextual evidence for inland water observations, including changes that can support investigation of turbidity or water-surface conditions when suitable imagery is available.

Prototype limitation: the final build registers this source and exposes its provenance, but does not claim automated live Sentinel-2 retrieval. Satellite observations are episodic and contextual, not high-frequency telemetry.
